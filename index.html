Build my plan
What do I get with the membership?

Every plan includes every gym and home programme, the full Berri Learn library, nutrition targets and the food scanner, cycle and habit tracking, and every new programme we add. There is no higher tier to upgrade to.

How does the plan builder work?

You answer six questions: your goal, focus, training days, home or gym, experience and how you feel about cardio. Berri matches you to the right programme and split from there.

Monthly, quarterly or annual: which should I pick?

Every plan gets exactly the same app and everything inside it. The only difference is price: the longer you commit, the less you pay per day. Annual is the best value.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Beginner is one of the starting points in the plan builder, and every exercise comes with a demo and an explanation of why it is there.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Every route has home and gym options, and you choose your setup when you build your plan.

What is Berri Learn?

Short, science-first Deep Dives on fat loss, glutes and recomp. You pick the topic, unlock a new lesson every 1 to 7 days, and keep every one in your library.

Do I have to do cardio?

No, cardio is optional. When you build your plan you choose yes, only if necessary, or no thanks, and Berri works around your answer.

Will the plans work around my cycle?

Yes. The period tracker sits alongside your training so you can see how your cycle affects energy and performance.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, in a couple of taps from inside the app. No emails, no phone calls.
